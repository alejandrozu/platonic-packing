# Papers for the Polyhedra-in-Polyhedra journal

Four papers on the catalogue, all published in the Journal section of polyhedrainpolyhedra.wiki (Paper III as the
8 November author copy; Papers I, II and IV as the author versions of 10 November 2026, commit `4842e61986`). Until
the Q4 2026 issue is published, they may still receive small revisions.

| II | Width, facet and ball bounds: thirty optimal packings of prisms, tetrahedra, octahedra and four-dimensional polytopes, with proofs checked in Lean | 30 proven optimal | [`II-width-ball/width-ball.pdf`](II-width-ball/width-ball.pdf) |

Paper IV collects the work on s_d(2) = 2 in every dimension: the equivalent forms (frames, the largest cube in a
simplex with vertices on orthogonal axes), the reductions proved in Lean in every dimension (near step, off-column
step, pivot inequality, aligned step; every dimension reduced to the facet lemma, `FacetStep.lean`), the Helmert
conjecture (with Croft's theorem as a check), the routes that fail with verified counterexamples, and a numerical
search in dimensions 3 to 32. Papers I and II (revised 10 Nov 2026) point to it.

Paper III is the revised, restructured version of `docs/patterns-paper/` (parts: mechanisms, descriptive
patterns, extremal patterns, relations and dimensions 4–6, formalization), with two new figures for dimensions 4–6.
The optimality material of the old patterns paper now lives in Papers I and II.

## Lean links

Every theorem in the papers links to its Lean declaration at a fixed commit, so the links do not move when the
code changes. `scripts/papers/leanlinks.py <commit>` reads the Lean files at that commit, writes the line of every
declaration to `leanlinks.tex`, and checks that every `\lean{File}{name}` used in the papers exists (exit code 1 if
one is missing). Papers I–III use commit `e3d934be83`, whose CI run (build and axiom audit) passed. Paper IV pins its
own commit (`--out docs/papers/leanlinks-iv.tex --papers 'IV-*'`), the first one with `FacetStep.lean`.

## Build

```
python3 scripts/papers/leanlinks.py e3d934be831e9878655d2999fbd6e8fe20d7ffeb
python3 scripts/papers/leanlinks.py <Paper IV commit> --out docs/papers/leanlinks-iv.tex --papers 'IV-*'
python3 scripts/papers/figs_paper1.py     # Paper I figures
python3 scripts/papers/figs_paper2.py     # Paper II figures (each construction is checked numerically)
python3 scripts/papers/figs_paper3.py     # Paper III new figures (from docs/patterns/*_nd.json)
python3 scripts/papers/figs_paper4.py <evidence commit>   # Paper IV tables, numbers, figures (from the evidence JSON)
cd docs/papers/I-cube-centre  && latexmk -pdf cube-centre.tex
cd docs/papers/II-width-ball  && latexmk -pdf width-ball.tex
cd docs/papers/III-patterns   && latexmk -pdf patterns.tex
cd docs/papers/IV-all-dimensions && latexmk -pdf all-dimensions.tex
```

The numerical evidence of Paper IV (data at commit `5c9e0344b8`, Lean links at `5832d6c232`) is produced by `scripts/optimality/lemmaF_all_d/evidence/run_all.py` and
`run_more.py` (two workers, resumable; about five hours on two cores) and `analysis.py`, `nearid.py`,
`forms_check.py`.

Paper III reads its catalogue numbers, tables and older figures from `docs/patterns-paper/` (generated by
`scripts/patterns/`), so every number has one source.
