Certified three-dimensional witnesses from the generalized pipeline: (a) 12 pentagonal prisms, (b) eight truncated octahedra, and (c) four pentagonal hexecontahedra, each in a homothetic copy of itself. Colors distinguish pieces; the grey wireframes show the containers. Positions and orientations are taken directly from the frozen full-precision records. The displayed scales are rounded; the certified full scales are given in Table 2 and the accompanying witness files. Panels (a) and (b) were obtained from symmetry-constrained starts followed by unconstrained tightening, and panel (c) from insertion and relaxation.

Rendering validation: canonical native vertices were read from src/solids_hp.json, with faces and edges from src/solids.json. Each scalar-first quaternion was evaluated as H(q)/||q||^2, matching certify_general.py; no search-unit normalization was applied. Containers use s_full times canonical vertices, and any native container_center is removed uniformly from the complete scene. All three centers are zero. The largest rotation orthogonality error was 4.45e-16; the largest rigid edge-length error was 5.56e-16; all displayed vertices have positive numerical containment slack of at least 1.00000007e-7. These numerical display checks are separate from the archived exact certificates. Colors identify piece index, not orientation classes. No geometry was spread, jittered or changed. PDF was rasterized with Poppler and visually inspected after generation.
