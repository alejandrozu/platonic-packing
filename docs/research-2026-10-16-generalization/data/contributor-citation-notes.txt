The accompanying repository serves as an open, versioned research archive for the project: it publishes geometry definitions, full coordinate witnesses, exact certificates, provenance, search code and research notes, and accepts independently developed constructions through a documented contribution route [repoArchive2026]. Its research material includes a literature review, an audit of historical coordinate sources, an empirical comparison of search moves, a large-n study of crystal cuts and tightening, and implementation and benchmark notes for dimensions four to six [repoRelated2026,repoAudit2026,repoSearch2026,repoLarge2026,repoHigher2026]. These materials document the evidence behind the implementation and connect individual records to the earlier literature; they are project research notes rather than a comprehensive survey of worldwide results.

The archive combines the project's searches with attributed external and community witnesses. These include PingYou Ltd's constructive submissions and higher-dimensional inclusion, subdivision and face-lift constructions [existing PingYou references]; Stian's octahedron-in-cube improvements for n=13,15,18 [Stian2026]; konoha-ily2's exact rational cube-in-cube witnesses [Konoha2026]; Ryan Xu's independently published n=34,35,36 cube packings [RyanXu2026]; the coordinate reconstructions of Friedman's n=13,14 cube constructions by @BuilderOfAgents (tronford) [Builder2026]; and Sam Scott's tightening audit of Haowei Lin's previously published configurations [Scott2026]. Original construction credit, imported-source lineage and later certificate refinement are retained separately. The displayed catalogue therefore represents the combined archive, with each selected case traceable to its source and verification files.
