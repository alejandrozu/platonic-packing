# Verified publication routes for 12 October 2026

**Checked 12 October 2026, Europe/Paris. Public platform rules only.**

**Zenodo is the strongest verified route for a public release in this session:** its normal direct-publication workflow does not await an editor or community curator, welcomes independent depositors, and assigns a DOI on publication. Figshare also documents author publication and DOI assignment for personal accounts. Neither service guarantees uninterrupted access, freedom from anti-spam checks, or permanent acceptance of every submission. A successful public record, downloadable files, and resolving DOI are the evidence of completion—not merely an upload, saved form, or reserved identifier.

HAL, arXiv, Preprints.org, SSRN, Research Square, and peer-reviewed journals have gates controlled by somebody else. Their forms can be submitted when author eligibility and account access are satisfied, but a public result tonight cannot be promised. OSF's generalist preprint server remains closed to new submissions. ResearchGate adds an account-admission gate for unaffiliated researchers. The updated journal check also identifies a material restriction affecting the earlier Experimental Mathematics recommendation.

This report contains no private correspondence, email addresses, credentials, or account identifiers. Authenticated account findings are kept separately. No upload, saved deposit, DOI reservation, external submission, or message was made as part of this research.

## Zenodo: requirements for a direct public release

[General policy](https://about.zenodo.org/policies/) accepts all research fields and output types, permits anyone to register, and requires the depositor to hold the necessary rights. [Terms of Use](https://about.zenodo.org/terms/) provide free uploading and retain CERN's ability to restrict accounts or remove unsuitable content. Neither institutional employment nor an arXiv-style endorser is a depositor requirement.

The [quick-start instructions](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/get-started/quickstart/) explain Upload → Describe → Publish and automatic DOI assignment. More explicitly, the [platform's community-workflow documentation](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/about/whats-new/) says the author can publish immediately without submitting to a community. Choosing a community review request instead adds a curator-controlled gate. Select public visibility for both metadata and files if the intended outcome is a publicly readable paper; a restricted file or embargo does not meet that objective.

[Record guidance](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/about-records/) distinguishes metadata, files, and the persistent identifier. The [deposit API specification](https://developers.zenodo.org/) confirms that creator affiliation and ORCID are optional. It requires the scientific description and publication metadata appropriate to the resource. Prepare the two PDFs with title, full author name, abstract, resource type **Publication / Preprint**, accurate version/publication date, language, keywords, and a chosen licence. Add repository/source-version links and a relationship to the reproducibility archive.

The [current size-limit FAQ](https://support.zenodo.org/help/en-gb/1-upload-deposit/80-what-are-the-size-limitations-of-zenodo) explicitly says there is **no quota on the number of records**, while imposing fair usage. The normal limit is **50 GB and 100 files per record**. A ZIP archive is appropriate for hundreds of configuration files. Two substantive papers and one related research archive are qualitatively different from bulk promotional deposits; the scientific content still has to comply with policy.

### AI tools and the scholarly basis

The [depositor generative-AI policy](https://support.zenodo.org/help/en-gb/13-policies/227-what-is-your-usage-policy-for-generative-ai-for-depositors), published 27 April 2026, requires a verifiable human research basis and author accountability. It permits tools for code, analysis, visualization, language, and organizing research, while rejecting unsupported manufactured findings. AI tools cannot be creators or contributors. Substantive use should be described **in the record description**, including tool, purpose, and affected content. Methodology disclosure inside the PDF remains useful but does not replace that metadata expectation.

The underlying experiments, coordinate archive, certificate replay, and reproducible numerical analysis are the research basis for these papers. The author must personally assess the interpretation and hypotheses before publication; automated verification of geometry does not validate every scientific sentence. The record description should describe the actual workflow accurately rather than imply that tools only corrected spelling.

[Unsuitable-content guidance](https://support.zenodo.org/help/en-gb/2-safelisting-spam/141-what-content-do-you-consider-spam) excludes research-free AI content and promotional material. [The false-positive FAQ](https://support.zenodo.org/help/en-gb/2-content/143-what-if-i-was-wrongly-blocked-for-spam) confirms that automatic and manual moderation can block legitimate material. An existing functioning account reduces one uncertainty; it does not bypass those controls. If a filter prevents publication, the truthful outcome is blocked or unpublished, with no claim that a DOI release is complete.

### Licence and citation choices

Use a paper licence the author can actually grant, such as CC BY 4.0 if selected. The repository software and viewer carry MIT notices crediting Yohei Nakajima and Alejandro Zarzuelo; preserve those notices in a software archive rather than silently changing their attribution. Existing external figures or full papers should not be copied into the release without rights. Links and original figures generated from the recorded coordinates serve the research description.

A reserved DOI is not registered until publication. An existing DOI for the same object should be supplied instead of minting another; a genuinely distinct software/data archive has its own identity. Use an exact-version DOI for the configuration archive analyzed by the papers, and cross-link companion papers and data. No DOI was reserved in this check.

## Figshare: direct publication, with important identity boundaries

Figshare's [personal-account terms](https://figshare.com/terms) allow individual accounts for the author's own non-commercial research. Its [researcher page](https://info.figshare.com/researchers/) describes free individual storage and DOI-equipped outputs. A university account or institution's review workflow should not be conflated with the personal repository.

The current [metadata/publication guide](https://info.figshare.com/user-guide/how-to-fill-in-the-metadata-fields-first-step/) specifies **Publish**, while institutional review can instead show **Submit for review**. Required fields are marked in the live form; prepare title, author, item type, description, relevant categories/keywords, and licence. Its [item-type page](https://info.figshare.com/user-guide/item-types/) includes **Preprint** as a default type for a manuscript not yet accepted for publication.

[Platform policies](https://info.figshare.com/user-guide/figshare-policies/) say personal content is moderated and disallow content already published with an existing DOI, unauthorized copyrighted material, and promotion. Thus Figshare should be an alternative primary DOI host or a host for a distinct research object, not a second new DOI for the identical paper after Zenodo publication. These rules do not prohibit an unpublished authored manuscript merely because the file is a PDF; the explicit Preprint item type establishes its role.

The checked terms and policy page did **not** provide a dedicated generative-AI permission or prohibition. That absence is not a blanket assurance. Preserve truthful tools disclosure and the verifiable research basis. Current [account limits](https://help.figshare.com/article/figshare-account-limits) specify 20 GB storage, 20 GB per file, 500 items, and 500 files per item. There is no verified month-specific two-paper cap in that limits table.

**Correction to a potential overstatement:** direct publication and DOI capability are documented; current account eligibility, a successful file upload, and an actual public DOI remain to be demonstrated. Figshare is not needed if Zenodo's already accessible direct workflow succeeds.

## Services where we can submit but cannot control public timing

### HAL

HAL's [deposit instructions](https://doc.hal.science/en/deposit_new/) require document type, title, discipline, full-text language, authors, and at least one affiliation. The [author/affiliation guide](https://doc.hal.science/en/author-and-affiliation/) describes research-structure affiliation at the time the work was produced. A name in an author profile or possession of a HAL account does not establish acceptance of new full-text deposits.

[Manual checking](https://doc.hal.science/en/check-of-deposits/) applies to **all full-text deposits** and can take a few days. Metadata-only references can appear immediately, but posting one would not publish the papers. The central documentation does not establish a universal independent-author exemption. Where account eligibility has not been cleared, HAL is not a confirmed submission path, much less a public release tonight. A previously requested correction must be resolved honestly rather than bypassed with a fabricated affiliation.

The checked core pages establish rights/coauthor-consent and document checks, but no detailed current depositor AI rule was located. This is an unresolved policy detail, not permission to conceal tool use. HAL's optional arXiv transfer does not remove arXiv's endorsement or moderation gates.

### Preprints.org

[Instructions for Authors](https://www.preprints.org/instructions-for-authors) cover mathematics, original articles, technical notes, and hypotheses; require author identity and contact information, bibliography, accessible supporting data, and substantive AI disclosure in Methods. Word or complete LaTeX sources are requested. An institution email is recommended rather than stated as universally mandatory, but the live independent-author field and account still need verification.

[The service description](https://www.preprints.org/about) confirms free posting, screening before publication, a Crossref DOI, and CC BY 4.0. Typical screening is under 24 hours or one business day; this is **not a promise of posting during the current session**. Excessive or similar submissions can be declined under the instructions. They discourage posting the identical paper on multiple preprint servers. If another primary host is selected tonight, do not automatically submit duplicates here as well.

### SSRN

SSRN's own [welcome statement](https://blog.ssrn.com/2025/11/20/welcome-to-ssrn/) expressly welcomes independent scholars without formal affiliation. Its [submission guidelines](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/get-started) require full-text scholarly research with rigorous methodology and original findings. Its [processing policy](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/ssrn-review-process), updated July 2026, says some submissions take up to ten business days.

[The AI policy](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/AI), updated August 2026, requires material tools disclosure inside the PDF, human verification, and human authorship. Manuscript-preparation support is permitted; fabricated findings and misrepresented contributions are not. This establishes the need for a truthful methodology account, not a particular result's acceptance. [DOI guidance](https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/doi) connects accepted preprints to stable identifiers and later journal versions. Submission is not scientific peer review or an immediate public record.

### arXiv

The [registration page](https://info.arxiv.org/help/registerhelp.html) explicitly allows **Independent** affiliation. The [endorsement rules](https://info.arxiv.org/help/endorsement.html) remain a separate gate for a first submission or new category. [Current moderation policy](https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html) states **two new submissions per calendar month**, **three active submissions**, and one replacement per week after version five. It also requires substantial AI tools use to be reported consistently with methodology standards and places full responsibility on human authors.

[Availability guidance](https://info.arxiv.org/help/availability.html) gives one to four days for checks, sometimes longer, and lists **Monday 12 October 2026** among deferred-mailing holidays. Public announcement and identifier assignment are scheduled; the identifier cannot be reserved in advance. Even an endorsed author with two slots cannot promise immediate public release in this session. The checked policy does not justify evading quotas through another person's account.

### OSF, Research Square, ResearchGate, and Authorea

The [OSF generalist suspension](https://help.osf.io/article/687-osf-preprints-suspension) continues for new deposits. [Community-server policy](https://help.osf.io/article/691-preprint-moderation-policies), updated August 2026, specifies prior moderation and excludes material completely or mostly generated by AI. A disciplinary community's scope must match the manuscript. OSF is not a generic alternative for immediate publication of these papers.

[Research Square's direct-submission guide](https://support.researchsquare.com/support/solutions/articles/6000271843-how-do-i-submit-a-preprint-) asks for primary institution information, manuscript metadata, and declarations, then editorial prescreening. It describes a typical 24–48-hour posting and DOI outcome. The independent-author exception and mathematical-paper acceptance criteria were not established by that page, so it is excluded from the confirmed tonight route rather than recommended on an assumption.

[ResearchGate's admission policy](https://help.researchgate.net/getting-started/signing-up-for-researchgate) normally verifies institutional email; unaffiliated applicants may face discretionary evidence review, with no promised outcome. Its [DOI tool](https://help.researchgate.net/research-and-publications/researchgate-dois) can register unpublished public full-text preprints and datasets, but says not to create another DOI for work already carrying one. This is useful for an existing eligible account, not evidence that a newly requested account will be active tonight.

[Wiley's Authorea description](https://authors.wiley.com/author-resources/Journal-Authors/Prepare/authorea.html) says it is free for researchers and usable by anyone. [Publishing documentation](https://authorea.crisp.help/en-us/category/publishing-zz8e81/) describes direct preprinting, licences, and DOI registration taking minutes to hours, with possible failures. Journal-integrated Under Review additionally depends on initial journal screening. Current authenticated direct-publishing access was not established here, and no platform-specific AI manuscript rule was verified. Those gaps prevent treating it as a better confirmed route than an accessible Zenodo account.

## Journal recognition: a correction to the earlier advice

**Experimental Mathematics remains a topical fit, but the present manuscripts cannot simply be called submission-ready under its publisher's current rules.** [Taylor & Francis's manuscript-preparation policy](https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/editorial-policies/using-ai-in-your-research-and-manuscript-preparations/) expressly prohibits AI-generated first drafts of a manuscript or its sections. It distinguishes permitted copyediting, translation, code assistance, and research uses. The current papers were drafted with AI from the author's research and notes; author review and a tools statement do not retroactively change that origin. This is a material correction to the earlier venue recommendation. No compliant submission was attempted or editorial exception assumed.

**Journal of Computational Geometry** is an established fee-free peer-review option. Its [journal statement](https://jocg.org/index.php/jocg/about) confirms diamond open access, DOIs, and peer review. Its [submission instructions](https://jocg.org/index.php/jocg/about/submissions) require LaTeX-prepared PDF, page and line numbers, appropriate references, author metadata, and exclusive journal consideration; they permit prior online posting. Its [review criteria](https://jocg.org/index.php/jocg/about/review-criteria) consider experimental and applied geometric contributions. No explicit AI drafting ban was found in those checked JoCG pages, but that is not advance editorial acceptance. Paper 1 is the stronger apparent fit for its geometric-method contribution.

**Discrete & Computational Geometry** explicitly covers packing, polytopes, configurations, and geometric algorithms in its [scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/454/aims-and-scope). Springer Nature's [current AI manuscript policy](https://www.springernature.com/gp/policies/editorial-policies/ai-manuscript-preparation) permits disclosed author-input drafting, requiring human-led arguments and accountability. The manuscript's intellectual interpretation must actually remain author-led. Neither journal promises completed review or publication tonight. A selected manuscript can be prepared for submission in parallel with an approved archival release; an acknowledgement is not accepted publication.

The fastest defensible outcome is therefore **public, inspectable, DOI-citable preprints and a reproducibility archive now**, followed by a journal chosen under its actual current rules. A repository's or publisher's recognizable name should not be used to imply that unreviewed material has received peer-reviewed validation.

## Evidence needed to call the release finished

For each paper, completion means a public record under its correct title and author, the intended licence and description, a downloadable final PDF, and a resolving DOI. The reproducibility record should identify the frozen commit and include the configurations, analysis, verifier, and relevant licences. An anonymous public-page check distinguishes actual dissemination from visibility only inside an authenticated upload dashboard.

This verification is the remaining operational step after an authorized publication. Form preparation and account inspection are useful, but they cannot be reported as external submission or publication. This report authorizes neither an upload nor a journal submission; it supplies the factual basis for choosing and completing the authorized route.
