Reproducibility archive for the two companion papers by Alejandro Zarzuelo Urdiales. It contains the frozen MIT-licensed source repository, 351 construction files with stored checks, the measurement tables and figures, exact verification and analysis scripts, manuscript sources, seven STL models, and an illustrated reference article with a rotating full-precision viewer. The reference article is not a published Wikipedia article. General global optimality is not claimed.

Tools used: Claude (Anthropic) assisted computational development under the author's direction; Codex assisted source inspection, reproducibility checks, quantitative analysis, and manuscript drafting and exposition. Computational tools are described in the methodology. Alejandro Zarzuelo Urdiales is the human author responsible for the work; no AI system is listed as an author.
