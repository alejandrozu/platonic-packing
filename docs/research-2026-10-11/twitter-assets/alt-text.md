# Suggested images and alt text

These new scientific graphics use the frozen certified pose files and full-precision metrics. They do not claim global optimality. PNGs are sized for social sharing; SVGs preserve editable vector output.

## 1. 01-nine-families.png

Nine rendered configurations from certified stored poses, arranged in a three-by-three grid. Unit pieces are solid colored polyhedra; enclosing containers are muted wireframes. The labeled piece-to-container pairs are cubes/cube n=12, tetrahedra/tetrahedron n=12, octahedra/octahedron n=12, dodecahedra/dodecahedron n=33, icosahedra/icosahedron n=12, cubes/octahedron n=12, octahedra/cube n=12, dodecahedra/icosahedron n=12 and icosahedra/dodecahedron n=12. Headline: Polyhedra in polyhedra packing. The archive contains 351 certified constructions across nine ordered families, all counts 2–40. This is the frozen certified study archive, not a current-best leaderboard. A better externally reported octahedra-in-cube n=12 bound lacks retrieved poses. The cube n=12 specimen reproduces Yohei Nakajima’s construction.

## 2. 02-dodecahedral-shell.png

Two views of the same certified 33-dodecahedron witness: left shows actual solid poses with translucent teal outer pieces, gold inner pieces, and a purple central piece; right shows the actual centers with explicitly schematic dashed shell guides that do not represent contacts. There is one central piece, 12 inner centers forming a dual icosahedral shell and 20 outer centers forming a dodecahedral shell. Counts 29–33 share certified container edge 3.7470909531576 by omitting outer pieces. The 33-piece near-contact graph has 102 edges and none connects similarly oriented pieces within 5 degrees. Colors denote shell class, not orientation. Feasible construction bounds, not a claim of global optimality.

## 3. 03-plateau-and-jump.png

A comparison of two actual archived patterns. Left: dodecahedra in a dodecahedron at counts 29–33 all have certified edge 3.7470909531576, while occupied volume rises through 55.1%, 57.0%, 58.9%, 60.8%, 62.7%. Right: six octahedra in an octahedron have certified edge 2.0000003674235; seven have 2.5000004286608. The edge rises 25.0% and container volume 95.3%, while occupied volume falls from 75.0% to 44.8%. Values are feasible construction upper bounds and their structural measurements, not proved global optima.
