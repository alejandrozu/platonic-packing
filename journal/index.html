Preprint2026-11-10

The cube centre lemma in dimensions three, four and five: side 2 is optimal for 2 to 2^d unit cubes, with proofs checked in Lean

Alejandro Zarzuelo Urdiales

Proves that side 2 is optimal for every number of unit cubes from 2 to 2^d in dimensions three, four and five, and that s_d(k^d) = k in every dimension. The lower bound is the cube centre lemma, reduced by Hosono to a matrix inequality (Lemma F); the proofs, including 1174 interval cases in dimension five, are formalized in Lean.

Preprint2026-11-10

Width, facet and ball bounds: thirty optimal packings of prisms, tetrahedra, octahedra and four-dimensional polytopes, with proofs checked in Lean

Alejandro Zarzuelo Urdiales

Proves thirty catalogue packings optimal, from prisms in prisms and cubes to two regular polytopes in four-dimensional containers. Each lower bound comes from one of five short arguments (volume, facet width, two balls, an inequality for orthogonal 3x3 matrices, box centres) and is attained by an explicit packing; all statements are formalized in Lean.

Preprint2026-11-10

Two unit cubes in a cube in every dimension: reductions checked in Lean, routes that fail, and the evidence for s_d(2) = 2

Alejandro Zarzuelo Urdiales

Collects the work on two unit cubes needing a container of side 2 in every dimension: equivalent forms, including the largest cube in a simplex with vertices on orthogonal axes; reductions proved in Lean that reduce every dimension to a single facet lemma; the Helmert conjecture, with Croft's tetrahedron theorem as a check; the routes that fail, with verified counterexamples; and a numerical search in dimensions 3 to 32 that finds no counterexample.

Preprint2026-11-08

Deletion, arrows, windows and composition: structural patterns in 10,404 certified polytope packings

Alejandro Zarzuelo Urdiales

Classifies eleven structural patterns across the 10,404-record catalogue in dimensions three to six. It distinguishes Lean-verified construction mechanisms, empirical descriptive patterns and extremal results, then studies their relationships, higher-dimensional rotations and contributor constructions.

Editorially reviewed article2026-10-27

Certified architecture transfer from polyhedral packing catalogues to finite-cluster magnetic response

Alejandro Zarzuelo Urdiales

A computational materials benchmark transfers a credited 28-cube packing into an uncalibrated finite-cluster equilibrium magnetic model. Controlled architecture, orientation and compression comparisons distinguish packing density from the modelled response. The study separates exact geometry, numerical validation and physical assumptions.
