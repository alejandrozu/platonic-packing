The cube centre lemma in dimensions three, four and five: side 2 is optimal for 2 to 2^d unit cubes, with proofs checked in Lean

Alejandro Zarzuelo Urdiales

Author manuscript

Read the PDFLaTeX source, figures and Lean link tableDownload citation

Overview

Proves that side 2 is optimal for every number of unit cubes from 2 to 2^d in dimensions three, four and five, and that s_d(k^d) = k in every dimension. The lower bound is the cube centre lemma, reduced by Hosono to a matrix inequality (Lemma F); the proofs, including 1174 interval cases in dimension five, are formalized in Lean.
