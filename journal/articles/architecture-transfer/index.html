Certified architecture transfer from polyhedral packing catalogues to finite-cluster magnetic response

Alejandro Zarzuelo Urdiales

Journal of Alternative Theoretical Physics (JOATP), Volume 1, Issue 1

Source recordRead the PDFDownload citation

Overview

A computational materials benchmark transfers a credited 28-cube packing into an uncalibrated finite-cluster equilibrium magnetic model. Controlled architecture, orientation and compression comparisons distinguish packing density from the modelled response. The study separates exact geometry, numerical validation and physical assumptions.
