Two unit cubes in a cube in every dimension: reductions checked in Lean, routes that fail, and the evidence for s_d(2) = 2

Alejandro Zarzuelo Urdiales

Author manuscript

Read the PDFLaTeX source, figures and Lean link tableDownload citation

Overview

Collects the work on two unit cubes needing a container of side 2 in every dimension: equivalent forms, including the largest cube in a simplex with vertices on orthogonal axes; reductions proved in Lean that reduce every dimension to a single facet lemma; the Helmert conjecture, with Croft's tetrahedron theorem as a check; the routes that fail, with verified counterexamples; and a numerical search in dimensions 3 to 32 that finds no counterexample.
