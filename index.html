How small a container can hold n copies of a polyhedron?

Smallest containers found so far for n = 2–100 copies of a solid: every Archimedean and Catalan solid in itself, regular prisms and antiprisms in themselves and in a cube, and (for n = 2–40) all 25 Platonic-solid piece/container pairs. 6,854 cases in 86 problems, with 9 attributed source-only entries and certified alternatives. Pick a family, a problem, then any n. Public constructions are included regardless of their author; where a better published result has no downloadable coordinates, the viewer shows an available coordinate construction at its own scale and links the published best separately.

How the project search works

The project search starts with soft pieces, sharpens them under pressure, then hops between arrangements. Independently published constructions retain their original methods and credit.

How coordinates are certified

Coordinate records carry exact certificates: coordinates in number fields, rational rotations and a separating plane for every pair of pieces. Source-only entries are labelled separately.

Beat a record

Paste a packing to check it in your browser, then submit it. A GitHub Action certifies it exactly and reports back.
