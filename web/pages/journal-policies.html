Journal policies

Open access, independent expert review and reproducible research for finite polyhedral and higher-dimensional polytope packing.

Volume 1, Issue 1, Q4 2026 is in preparation. These policies define the publication process for new Journal submissions. Papers indexed in the research library retain their original venue, version, license and review status.

Read the Journal · Submit a paper for editorial review

Scope

The Journal considers original research, methods, reproducibility reports, proof notes and reviews about finite packings of convex polyhedra in three dimensions and convex polytopes in higher dimensions. Relevant materials applications must identify the geometric construction and the physical model or experiment connecting it to a material response. Two-dimensional packing submissions are outside this Journal's scope.

Give the bodies, container, dimension, permitted motions, scale convention and precise claim. Credit the original construction, prior method and later refinements separately. A useful reconstruction or verification can be published on its own terms when its relationship to earlier work is explicit.

Independent peer review

A new paper receives the Journal's peer-reviewed label only after two independent expert reports, an author response addressing substantive concerns and a recorded acceptance decision by an unconflicted handling editor. No paper is accepted merely by appearing in the library or being submitted through GitHub.

  1. Editorial screening. Check scope, authorship, existing publication, rights, disclosures and the availability of evidence needed to examine the central claims.
  2. Expert assessment. Assign a handling editor and two reviewers with relevant expertise. Record their conflict checks. Reports assess originality, mathematical or physical reasoning, attribution and reproducibility.
  3. Response and revision. Authors receive the reports, answer the substantive points and identify their changes. A revised manuscript returns to review when unresolved claims or substantial changes require it.
  4. Decision and publication. The handling editor records acceptance, further revision or rejection with reasons. An accepted article receives a versioned public record and its issue assignment.

Review reports, responses and decisions are published only with the relevant authors' and reviewers' consent. The article record identifies the review basis, decision date and responsible editor. Private reviewer identities and contact details remain private unless their owners agree to disclosure. An AI-assisted assessment is disclosed and cannot replace either independent expert report.

Editorial responsibility and conflicts

The founding coordinator is Alejandro Zarzuelo Urdiales. Confirmed editorial appointments and their roles will be published in the Journal. A review invitation or an indexed paper does not constitute an editorial appointment.

Authors, editors and reviewers disclose relevant financial interests, affiliations, recent collaborations and personal or supervisory relationships. The handling editor records how a conflict is resolved before assigning a reviewer or making a decision. Authors cannot review or accept their own work.

A submission by the founding coordinator or another editor requires an independent handling editor and two independent reviewers. Its authors take no part in selecting the reviewers or deciding acceptance. Review waits until independent handling is available. The resulting article records how the conflict was managed.

Publication status and article records

Every listed work has a visible status. A deposited preprint has not thereby completed peer review. An article accepted or reviewed elsewhere keeps that venue's stated review basis. A dataset or software release is identified as such. These records are distinct from papers accepted through this Journal's own process.

For a new Journal article, the public record gives the title, authors, abstract, version, publication date, rights, evidence links and review basis. Received, revised and accepted dates are added when those events occur. Earlier versions remain traceable. Indexing a paper does not assert a new first publication or transfer its copyright.

Evidence and reproducibility

Link a stable manuscript version and the coordinates, definitions, code, data or formal sources needed to examine the result. Identify commits or checksums, dependencies and commands where they determine the outcome. Explain any unavailable component and how its associated claim can be assessed.

A feasible packing supplies a constructive upper bound. Global optimality needs a matching lower bound or a separate proof. A floating-point check, exact certificate and Lean theorem have different scopes; the paper must state which inputs and conclusions each actually covers. An automatic packing check does not review an entire paper.

Materials studies distinguish experimental observations, numerical evidence and consequences of an assumed physical model. State calibration, uncertainty and validation limits beside the associated result. A geometric certificate alone does not establish material performance.

Disclose funding, relevant interests and substantive AI assistance. Authors remain responsible for the manuscript, citations and supplied evidence. Where possible, connect a result to its inspectable construction in the catalogue and explain its certificate through the certification guide.

Open access, rights and fees

Reading, submission and article processing are free. There are no submission fees or article processing charges. Any future printing or shipping arrangements are separate from review and editorial decisions.

Authors retain copyright. New manuscripts accepted by this Journal are published under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 with the authors' explicit agreement. Third-party content requires compatible permission or a clear exception notice. Software and data retain their declared licenses.

Externally published papers and earlier deposits retain their original licenses. The Journal can link to an authorized publisher or repository copy without republishing the file. A paper's DOI continues to identify the specific work and version registered by its original provider.

Issues and the planned print edition

The planned schedule combines rolling online publication with quarterly issues. Volume 1, Issue 1 covers Q4 2026 and is still in progress. New Journal articles join an issue after completing review and acceptance. Issue covers, contents and article versions will remain available together.

A printed edition for distribution to academics and research libraries worldwide is planned. Print quantities, production dates, prices and shipping arrangements have not opened. Registering interest is not an order and creates no payment or delivery commitment.

Register institutional or print interest. Use that public form without a postal address or payment details. Delivery information, if needed later, will be requested through a private route before any shipment is arranged.

Corrections, withdrawal and reconsideration

Report an error through a GitHub issue, identifying the paper, version, location and supporting evidence. Keep private correspondence and personal information out of public issues. A correction receives a dated notice linked to the affected version; a substantial change may require renewed review.

A withdrawal or retraction leaves a public status record explaining the decision and identifying the affected version. The handling editor considers an author's reasoned request for reconsideration. When that editor is conflicted, a separate unconflicted editor handles the request.

Identifiers and editorial enquiries

No ISSN, Journal-issued DOI or indexing registration is claimed at launch. Assigned identifiers and confirmed indexing services will be displayed when available. Existing DOI and arXiv links refer to the original records.

For editorial consideration, use the Journal submission form. The issue is public and is submitted only when you choose to send it through GitHub. Supply public manuscript and evidence links, author approval and disclosures. Submission starts editorial consideration; it is not acceptance.

To submit coordinates for the record book, use Contribute instead. Journal editorial submissions and packing-record certification follow separate processes.
